About

Welcome to Writing Style Analyzer, a free online tool designed to help English writers understand, dissect, and refine their writing patterns.

Our mission is to provide you with actionable, granular insights into your prose. We believe that visualizing your rhythm and vocabulary is the first step toward becoming a more effective communicator.

What Makes Us Different?

  1. 100% Private & Local: Your text never leaves your browser. All analysis runs entirely via client-side JavaScript on your local device. We don't use databases, we don't store your writing, and no one else will ever read your input.
  2. Instant Visualization: Get immediate interactive charts detailing your sentence length distribution and word frequency statistics.

Who is this for?

Whether you are a student polishing an essay, a novelist engineering your prose rhythm, or a professional refining your business communication, Writing Style Analyzer provides the data you need to elevate your craft.
