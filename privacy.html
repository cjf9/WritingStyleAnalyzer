Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

1. Overview

Writing Style Analyzer ("the Service," "we," "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

2. Text Analysis — Fully Local

Your text never leaves your device. All analysis — sentence splitting, lemmatization, frequency computation, and chart rendering — runs entirely in your browser using client-side JavaScript. We have no server-side storage, no database, and no ability to read, access, or retain any text you submit. When you close the page, your data is gone.

3. Google Analytics (GA4)

We use Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID: G-694HG8WN72) to understand site traffic in aggregate. GA4 collects:

  1. Page views and navigation patterns
  2. Traffic sources (e.g., search engine, direct, social)
  3. Broad geographic region (country/city level, derived from IP)
  4. Browser type, device category, and screen resolution

This data is aggregated and anonymized — we cannot identify individual users. GA4 uses first-party cookies for this purpose. Google may process this data according to its own privacy policy.

4. Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements on the Service. AdSense uses cookies and web beacons to collect information about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide advertisements that may be of interest to you. This may include:

  1. Ad impressions and interactions
  2. Browser and device information
  3. Interest-based advertising data

Google uses the DoubleClick cookie to enable interest-based advertising. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You may also opt out of third-party cookies for personalized advertising at AboutAds.info.

5. Google Fonts

We load fonts (Baloo 2, Inter) from Google Fonts for visual presentation. When your browser requests these fonts, Google receives your IP address. Google's Privacy Policy governs that data.

6. Cookies

Cookies set through our site fall into these categories:

  1. Analytics cookies (GA4) — used to measure site traffic in aggregate. See Section 3.
  2. Advertising cookies (Google AdSense) — used to serve relevant ads and measure ad performance. See Section 4.

We do not set any other cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. Disabling cookies will not affect the functionality of the text analysis tool.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes. The third-party services we use (Google Analytics, Google AdSense) process data according to their own policies. No user-submitted text is ever shared with any third party.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since all text analysis runs locally in the browser, children who use the Service do not transmit any data to us — simply closing the page removes all data.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights under GDPR (EU/EEA), CCPA (California), or equivalent laws. The only "personal data" we process is the limited analytics and advertising data described in Sections 3 and 4. Your submitted text never reaches us. For questions, contact us at WritingStyleAnalyzer@hotmail.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect immediately upon posting. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
